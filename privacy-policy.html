1. Introduction

Squarredesk (“we”, “us”) builds tools that help small businesses get online and run their business, including website building, collaboration, finance and supplier tools. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect about the people who use Squarredesk and visit our website, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

“Personal information” means information that identifies you or can reasonably be used to identify you, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (“POPIA”). For the personal information we collect from you directly, we are the “responsible party” under POPIA. This Privacy Policy forms part of our Terms of Service. If we make significant changes to it, we will let you know by email or in the product.

2. Our values

Squarredesk exists so small businesses can start on an even playing field. Three ideas guide how we treat your information.

Your information belongs to you

The websites, designs and business records you create on Squarredesk are yours. We use your personal information to run the product for you, not to profit from it.

We collect only what we need

We try to ask only for the information we need to provide Squarredesk, and we delete it when we no longer need it.

We keep things clear

We aim to explain how we use your information in plain language, and to tell you when that changes.

3. Why we process your information

We process personal information to run Squarredesk and give you the service you signed up for. The information we collect falls into these groups:

  • Account information — your name, email address, login details and, where you give them to us, your phone number and business name.
  • Business and Content information — the websites, designs, documents, finance records and other material you create, upload or store using Squarredesk, and the details you provide about your business.
  • Payment information — what we need to confirm and keep a record of your payments. Card details are entered with, and processed by, our payment provider PayFast.
  • Usage and device information — your IP address, browser and device type, and how you use our website and apps.
  • Communications — messages you send to our team and your email preferences.

POPIA requires us to have a lawful reason for processing your information. We rely on these reasons to:

  • provide, maintain and support your account and Squarredesk, and contact you about your account (to carry out our agreement with you);
  • process payments and keep financial records (to carry out our agreement with you and to comply with the law);
  • keep Squarredesk secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix problems (our legitimate interests, and those of our users);
  • understand how Squarredesk is used so we can improve it (our legitimate interests);
  • send you news, offers and updates (only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails).

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your rights over your information

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access — ask whether we hold personal information about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correction — ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete personal information we no longer need or are not entitled to keep.
  • Objection — object to us processing your information for our legitimate interests, or for direct marketing.
  • Withdrawing consent — withdraw any consent you have given, at any time.

To use any of these rights, email us at [privacy email]. We may need to verify your identity first to protect your information, and we will respond within a reasonable time, aiming for 30 days. These rights are not absolute, and we may need to keep some information where the law requires it.

If you gave your information to a business that uses Squarredesk to run its website, that business decides how it is used. Please contact the business directly about it.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at any time.

5. Where we send your information

We share personal information only when it is needed to run Squarredesk. Some of it may be sent to service providers (“subprocessors”) that help us deliver the product. For example:

  • Payments — when you pay, the information needed to complete your payment is sent to our payment provider, PayFast, which handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and infrastructure — cloud providers that store and serve your account, Content and websites.
  • Email and analytics — providers that help us send messages and understand how Squarredesk is used.

Our subprocessors may only use your information to provide their service to us, and we require them to protect it. Some of them are based outside South Africa, so your information may be sent to other countries. When that happens, we take steps to make sure it stays protected, as POPIA requires.

We may also share information where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order. We will tell you first where we are allowed to.

6. How long do we retain your information

We keep your personal information for as long as you are a Squarredesk user. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and Content.

There are limited exceptions. We may keep information that the law requires us to retain, such as financial and payment records, and for a short time it may remain in backups until they are cleared on their regular schedule.

To delete your account, contact us at [privacy email].

7. How we protect your information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. These include encrypted connections, access controls that limit who can see your information, and written agreements with our subprocessors.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can help by choosing a strong, unique password and keeping it private. If a security breach puts your personal information at risk, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. How we use “cookies” and other tracking technologies

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device. We use cookies and similar technologies for these purposes:

  • Essential — to keep you signed in and keep Squarredesk secure. The product cannot work without these.
  • Preferences — to remember your settings and choices.
  • Analytics — to understand how our website and product are used, so we can improve them.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block essential cookies, parts of Squarredesk may stop working.
