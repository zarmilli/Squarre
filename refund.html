1. Overview

This Refund Policy explains when Squarredesk issues refunds and how to ask for one. It applies to subscription payments and to purchases from the Squarredesk supplier marketplace, and it forms part of our Terms of Service.

In short: subscriptions are generally not refundable, marketplace purchases are not refundable because the items are customized, and we will always correct genuine billing errors.

2. Subscriptions

Squarredesk subscriptions are billed in advance for each billing period. We generally do not offer refunds on subscriptions, including for unused time, partial billing periods or plan downgrades.

You can cancel at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again after that. If your plan renews automatically, cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

3. When we issue refunds

We will review your request and, where it is valid, refund you in these cases:

  • Double charging — you were charged more than once for the same billing period or order.
  • Charging after termination — you were charged for a period that began after your account was closed or terminated.
  • Incorrect charging — you were charged the wrong amount, for a plan you did not select, or at a price different from the one you agreed to.

In these cases we refund the amount charged in error, including any VAT charged on it.

Other situations

If your situation is not listed above, you can still contact us. We may, at our discretion, agree to a full or partial refund where we reach a reasonable agreement with you through communication and there is a valid reason. We look at each request on its own merits, and agreeing to one request does not oblige us to do the same for others.

What usually does not qualify

  • Changing your mind or no longer needing the service.
  • Forgetting to cancel before a renewal.
  • Not using your account or its features.
  • Unused time left after you cancel.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for breaking the Terms of Service.
  • Bank, card or currency fees charged by third parties.

4. Supplier marketplace purchases

Items from the supplier marketplace are customized to your specifications, so we do not issue refunds for marketplace purchases. Please check the item details, quantities and customizations carefully before you pay.

If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible. We can only help if the supplier has not yet started work on it, and we cannot guarantee that a change or cancellation will be possible.

This does not apply to billing errors, which are covered in Section 3, and it does not affect any rights you have by law, for example if an item is not delivered or is defective (see Section 7).

5. How to request a refund

Email us at [billing email] and include:

  • The email address on your Squarredesk account.
  • The date and amount of the charge, and the payment or order reference if you have it.
  • A short explanation of what went wrong and what you are asking for. Screenshots help.

Please contact us within 30 days of the charge. We will acknowledge your request and aim to reply within 5 business days. We may ask for more information, and we will tell you our decision and the reasons for it.

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. It is usually the faster way to fix a problem.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid back to your original payment method through our payment provider, PayFast, in the same currency as the original charge. How long a refund takes to appear depends on PayFast and your bank, and it can take several business days.

We refund only the amount charged in error or the amount we agreed with you. We cannot refund fees charged by your bank.

7. Your legal rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits the rights you have under South African consumer law, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2008 and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2002 (“ECTA”).

Where ECTA’s cooling-off right applies to your purchase, you may cancel within seven days without giving a reason. That right does not apply to some transactions, including services that began with your consent before the seven days ended and goods made to your specifications.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version on this page applies to purchases made while it is in effect, and we will tell you about significant changes by email or in the product.

Questions about this policy? Email us at [billing email].
