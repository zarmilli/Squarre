About the Opportunity

Squarredesk is seeking an experienced, commercially minded and strategically driven Senior Wealth & Financial Advisor to oversee the company's financial resources, asset allocation and investment strategy. This is a senior-level opportunity for a finance professional with a strong understanding of wealth management, corporate finance, treasury operations and investment portfolio management. The successful candidate will play a critical role in managing Squarredesk's financial position, ensuring that available capital is allocated strategically, corporate assets are managed responsibly and financial resources are positioned to support long-term growth. Working closely with company leadership, you will contribute to financial planning, capital preservation, investment decisions and the development of sustainable wealth creation strategies. We are looking for someone who understands that financial management extends beyond maintaining accounts and monitoring expenditure. It requires strategic thinking, sound judgement and the ability to identify opportunities that strengthen the company's financial position. This position comes with a premium remuneration package, executive-level benefits and the opportunity to influence the financial direction of a growing technology company.

Job Description

As our Senior Wealth & Financial Advisor, you will be responsible for overseeing Squarredesk's financial assets, treasury operations and capital allocation strategy.

Your responsibilities will include:

1. Wealth & Asset Management

  • Overseeing the management and allocation of Squarredesk's financial assets.
  • Developing investment strategies that balance capital preservation, liquidity and long-term returns.
  • Evaluating investment opportunities across appropriate asset classes and financial instruments.
  • Conducting financial research, market analysis and investment performance assessments.
  • Identifying opportunities to grow and diversify the company's financial portfolio.
  • Monitoring investment performance and making recommendations on portfolio adjustments.

2. Treasury & Cash Management

  • Managing corporate cash reserves, liquidity and cash allocation.
  • Developing strategies for optimising available cash while maintaining sufficient operational liquidity.
  • Evaluating suitable banking products, interest-bearing accounts and investment instruments.
  • Monitoring cash inflows, outflows and working capital requirements.
  • Establishing appropriate cash reserve policies and liquidity management procedures.
  • Managing banking relationships and evaluating financial service providers.

3. Financial Planning & Strategy

  • Developing long-term financial strategies aligned with Squarredesk's business objectives.
  • Advising leadership on capital allocation, financial planning and resource optimisation.
  • Preparing financial forecasts, projections and investment scenarios.
  • Supporting annual budgeting and financial planning processes.
  • Evaluating major financial commitments and their potential impact on the company's financial position.
  • Identifying financial risks and developing appropriate mitigation strategies.

4. Financial Reporting & Governance

  • Preparing periodic financial performance and investment reports for company leadership.
  • Maintaining oversight of corporate assets and investment records.
  • Establishing financial monitoring procedures and internal controls.
  • Ensuring appropriate documentation and reporting of financial transactions.
  • Monitoring relevant financial regulations, market developments and compliance requirements.
  • Providing management with actionable insights to support informed financial decisions.

Role Requirements

Candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • A bachelor's degree in Finance, Financial Management, Investment Management, Economics, Accounting or a related field.
  • At least 5 years of relevant professional experience in wealth management, corporate finance, treasury management, investment management or financial advisory.
  • Strong understanding of financial markets, investment instruments and portfolio management.
  • Demonstrated experience in cash management, liquidity planning and capital allocation.
  • Ability to evaluate investment opportunities and conduct financial risk assessments.
  • Strong financial modelling, forecasting and analytical capabilities.
  • Understanding of corporate financial governance, reporting and internal controls.
  • Excellent numerical, analytical and strategic decision-making skills.
  • Strong communication and presentation skills, particularly when presenting financial recommendations to senior management.
  • High levels of professionalism, confidentiality and financial integrity.
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating with executive leadership.

Good to Have

The following qualifications, skills and experience will be advantageous:

  • Professional qualifications such as CFA, CFP, CA(SA), CIMA or equivalent.
  • Postgraduate qualifications in Finance, Investment Management or Business Administration.
  • Experience managing corporate investment portfolios or treasury functions.
  • Experience working with institutional investors, asset managers, private banks or financial institutions.
  • Knowledge of South African and international investment markets.
  • Familiarity with money market instruments, fixed-income securities, equities, ETFs and alternative investments.
  • Experience developing corporate investment policies and treasury frameworks.
  • Knowledge of tax-efficient investment structures and corporate financial planning.
  • Experience advising executive leadership or company directors on financial strategy.
  • Strong proficiency in financial modelling and analytical software.
