About the Opportunity

Squarredesk is looking for an energetic, persuasive and results-driven Lead Generation Specialist to help expand our reach, introduce our platform to new audiences and generate valuable business opportunities. We are looking for someone with strong interpersonal skills, a creative approach to sales and the confidence to engage with potential customers both in person and through direct outreach. As a Lead Generation Specialist, you will represent Squarredesk in the field and across various outreach channels. From business canvassing and promotional activations to exhibitions, networking events and direct prospect engagement, your role will be centred around introducing people to Squarredesk, generating interest and converting that interest into qualified leads. You'll be provided with branded uniforms, marketing materials and the necessary working resources to support your outreach activities. This is a performance-driven, commission-based opportunity suited to someone who enjoys interacting with people, identifying business opportunities and taking initiative.

Job Description

As our Lead Generation Specialist, you will be responsible for identifying, approaching and generating potential customers for Squarredesk through creative outreach and promotional activities.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Identifying and approaching potential customers, entrepreneurs, startups and small businesses who could benefit from Squarredesk.
  • Conducting direct business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2C) outreach.
  • Canvassing designated areas to introduce Squarredesk to local businesses and prospective customers.
  • Distributing promotional materials, including pamphlets, flyers and other branded marketing resources.
  • Representing Squarredesk at exhibitions, trade shows, business events and promotional activations.
  • Engaging prospective customers through face-to-face interactions, networking and direct communication.
  • Developing creative approaches and sales techniques to generate interest and encourage platform registrations.
  • Explaining Squarredesk's products, features and value proposition to prospective customers.
  • Identifying qualified leads and maintaining accurate records of outreach activities and generated prospects.
  • Following up with interested prospects to nurture potential conversions.
  • Meeting agreed lead generation and conversion targets.
  • Providing feedback on customer responses, market opportunities and outreach effectiveness.

Role Requirements

Candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • A qualification or recognised certificate in Marketing, Sales, Business Administration, Communications or a related field.
  • Good verbal communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Confidence in approaching and engaging unfamiliar people in professional settings.
  • Strong persuasion, negotiation and relationship-building abilities.
  • Understanding of basic sales principles, prospecting and lead generation.
  • Ability to identify potential customers and communicate product value effectively.
  • Good organisational skills and the ability to maintain accurate lead records.
  • Self-motivation, resilience and a target-driven mindset.
  • Willingness to travel for canvassing, exhibitions, promotional events and other outreach activities.
  • Ability to work independently and manage field activities responsibly.

Good to Have

The following skills and experience will be advantageous:

  • Previous experience in sales, lead generation, direct marketing or business development.
  • Experience with field marketing, promotional activations and business canvassing.
  • Experience representing brands at exhibitions, trade shows and networking events.
  • Familiarity with CRM systems and lead management tools.
  • Experience selling or promoting software, SaaS products, technology platforms or business services.
  • Basic understanding of digital marketing and online business platforms.
  • Experience developing creative sales pitches and customer acquisition strategies.
  • Existing knowledge of the small business and entrepreneurship landscape.

Compensation & Benefits

This is a 100% commission-based opportunity. There is no fixed monthly base salary.

Your earnings will be determined by the qualified conversions generated through your lead generation and outreach activities.

  • Performance-Based Earnings: Earn commission for every verified conversion attributable to your lead generation activities.
  • Branded Uniform: Squarredesk-branded uniform will be provided for professional representation during outreach activities and events.
  • Working Materials: Access to promotional materials, pamphlets, flyers, product information and other resources required for lead generation.
  • Hybrid Work Arrangement: A combination of remote work and physical outreach, including canvassing, exhibitions and promotional activities.
  • Growth Opportunities: An opportunity to develop practical experience in sales, marketing and business development while contributing to the growth of an expanding technology company.
