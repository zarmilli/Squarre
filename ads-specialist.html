About the Opportunity

Squarredesk is looking for a motivated, creative and performance-driven Ads Specialist to join our growing team. This is an opportunity for someone who understands digital advertising, enjoys experimenting with different marketing strategies and knows how to turn compelling content into measurable business results.

As an Ads Specialist, you will be responsible for creating, managing and optimising paid advertising campaigns across relevant digital platforms to drive customer acquisition, registrations and conversions for Squarredesk.

You’ll be provided with marketing materials, brand assets and campaign content to work with, giving you the flexibility to develop advertising creatives, test different approaches and build campaigns that deliver results.

This is a fully remote, commission-based opportunity suited to someone who is confident in their advertising abilities and enjoys having their earnings directly influenced by their performance.

Job Description

As our Ads Specialist, you will play an important role in expanding Squarredesk’s digital presence and growing its user base through paid advertising.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Creating and launching paid advertising campaigns across platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google Ads.
  • Developing advertising strategies using marketing materials and content provided by Squarredesk.
  • Identifying target audiences and implementing appropriate audience-targeting strategies.
  • Testing different ad creatives, copy, formats and campaign approaches to improve performance.
  • Monitoring campaign performance, analysing conversion data and optimising advertisements accordingly.
  • Driving measurable conversions, including platform registrations and other agreed acquisition objectives.
  • Managing advertising budgets responsibly and ensuring campaigns are cost-effective.
  • Staying informed about digital advertising trends, platform updates and emerging marketing opportunities.
  • Providing periodic campaign performance reports and insights.

Role Requirements

Candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • A qualification or recognised certificate in Marketing, Digital Marketing, Advertising or a related field.
  • At least 1 year of practical experience creating, running and managing paid advertising campaigns.
  • Working knowledge of advertising platforms, particularly Meta Ads Manager (Facebook and Instagram).
  • Understanding of audience segmentation, targeting, conversion tracking and campaign optimisation.
  • Ability to interpret advertising analytics and make data-driven decisions.
  • Good written communication skills, particularly for advertising copy and promotional messaging.
  • Self-motivation, accountability and the ability to work independently in a remote environment.
  • Access to a computer and a reliable internet connection.

Good to Have

The following skills and experience will be advantageous:

  • Graphic design experience, particularly using Canva, Photoshop or Illustrator.
  • Video editing skills for creating short-form advertising content.
  • Experience with TikTok Ads, Google Ads and other digital advertising platforms.
  • Basic knowledge of website analytics, SEO and conversion tracking.
  • Experience marketing SaaS products, technology platforms or subscription-based services.
  • Understanding of website development, ecommerce or the broader technology industry.
  • Experience developing marketing creatives and adapting content for different advertising platforms.

Compensation & Benefits

This is a 100% commission-based opportunity. There is no fixed monthly base salary.

Your earnings will be directly linked to the conversions generated through your advertising campaigns.

  • Performance-Based Earnings: Earn a predetermined commission for every verified conversion attributed to your advertisements.
  • Marketing Resources: Access to Squarredesk’s marketing materials, promotional content, brand assets and other resources to support your campaigns.
  • Data Allowance: A monthly data allowance of 10GB will be provided to support your remote working activities.
  • Flexible Remote Work: Work remotely and manage your advertising activities independently, subject to agreed campaign objectives and reporting requirements.
  • Growth Opportunities: An opportunity to grow alongside an expanding technology company and contribute to its customer acquisition strategy.
