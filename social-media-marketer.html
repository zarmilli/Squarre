About the Opportunity

Squarredesk is looking for a creative, enthusiastic and digitally savvy Junior Social Media Marketing Specialist to join our growing team.

We are looking for someone who understands the power of social media in building brands, engaging audiences and driving business growth. You’ll play an active role in shaping Squarredesk’s online presence, developing engaging content and building a community around our platform.

Working alongside the broader marketing team, you’ll help bring the Squarredesk brand to life across various social media platforms through creative campaigns, consistent content publishing and audience engagement.

This is an opportunity for someone who enjoys content creation, understands digital culture and wants to develop their marketing career within a growing technology company.

Job Description

As our Junior Social Media Marketing Specialist, you will be responsible for managing and growing Squarredesk’s social media presence across relevant platforms.

Your responsibilities will include:

  • Planning, creating and publishing social media content across platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok.
  • Developing and maintaining a consistent social media content calendar.
  • Creating engaging captions, posts, stories, reels and other platform-specific content.
  • Managing day-to-day social media interactions, including comments, messages and audience engagement.
  • Identifying relevant social media trends, topics and opportunities that align with the Squarredesk brand.
  • Developing creative ideas for social media campaigns, promotions and brand awareness initiatives.
  • Monitoring social media performance, tracking engagement metrics and preparing periodic reports.
  • Researching competitors, industry trends and audience behaviour to inform content strategies.
  • Maintaining brand consistency across all social media platforms and marketing communications.
  • Collaborating with the marketing team to support broader promotional campaigns and business objectives.

Role Requirements

Candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • A qualification or recognised certificate in Marketing, Digital Marketing, Social Media Marketing, Communications or a related field.
  • Basic practical experience managing social media accounts or developing content for brands, businesses or organisations.
  • Good understanding of major social media platforms, particularly Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn.
  • Understanding of social media engagement, content strategy, audience growth and platform-specific best practices.
  • Strong written communication skills, particularly in developing engaging captions and promotional content.
  • Creativity and an ability to identify opportunities for interesting and relevant content.
  • Good organisational skills and the ability to manage content schedules and deadlines.
  • Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fully remote environment.
  • Access to a computer and a reliable internet connection.

Good to Have

The following skills and experience will be advantageous:

  • Graphic design experience using Canva, Photoshop or Illustrator.
  • Video editing experience using CapCut, Premiere Pro or similar software.
  • Experience creating short-form video content, particularly Instagram Reels and TikTok videos.
  • Basic photography, videography and visual storytelling skills.
  • Experience using social media scheduling and management tools.
  • Familiarity with Meta Business Suite and social media analytics.
  • Basic understanding of SEO, digital advertising and online brand positioning.
  • Experience marketing SaaS products, technology platforms or ecommerce businesses.
  • Familiarity with website content management systems and digital marketing tools.

Compensation & Benefits

Squarredesk offers a competitive monthly base salary for this position, alongside the flexibility and benefits of a remote working arrangement.

  • Competitive Salary: A fixed monthly salary, with compensation determined by experience and suitability for the role.
  • Remote Work: Work from anywhere, with no daily office commute.
  • Data Allowance: A monthly data allowance of 10GB to support your remote working activities.
  • Creative Freedom: An opportunity to contribute ideas, experiment with content formats and help shape the company’s digital identity.
  • Professional Growth: Gain practical experience in digital marketing, brand development and social media strategy within a growing technology company.
