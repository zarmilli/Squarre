To accept payments at checkout, you connect your own PayFast merchant account to Squarredesk. This takes about 10 minutes once PayFast has verified your account.

  1. Step 1

    Create your PayFast merchant account

    Sign up for a merchant account on the PayFast website and complete their verification. PayFast may ask for personal or business and bank details to verify you, so have them ready.

  2. Step 2

    Find your Merchant ID and Merchant Key

    In your PayFast dashboard, open the integration (developer) settings. You’ll see your Merchant ID and Merchant Key there. Keep this page open, because you’ll need both in a moment.

  3. Step 3

    Set a security passphrase

    On the same PayFast page, create a passphrase and save it. It protects your payments, and it must match the one you enter in Squarredesk exactly, with no extra spaces.

  4. Step 4

    Connect PayFast in Squarredesk

    In Squarredesk, open Settings, then Payments, and choose PayFast. Paste in your Merchant ID, Merchant Key and passphrase, then save. Checkout is now switched on for your site.

  5. Step 5

    Make a test purchase

    Buy something small from your own site to confirm checkout works and the payment appears in Squarredesk and in your PayFast dashboard. PayFast also has a sandbox for test payments, and its credentials are different from your live ones, so use your live details when you’re ready to go live.

Seeing a signature or security error at checkout? The passphrase in Squarredesk probably doesn’t match the one saved in PayFast exactly. Copy it again from PayFast, paste it in, and check for stray spaces.

SEO helps people find your site on search engines like Google. These four steps cover the basics that make the biggest difference.

  1. Step 1

    Open your site’s SEO settings

    In Squarredesk, select your site and open its SEO settings. You can set these for the whole site and for each page.

  2. Step 2

    Write a page title and description

    The title is the headline people see in search results, and the description is the short summary beneath it. Aim for a title under about 60 characters and a description under about 160, and mention what you do and where.

  3. Step 3

    Use clear page URLs and image descriptions

    Give each page a short, readable URL, such as /about instead of /page-2. Add a short description (alt text) to your images, which helps search engines and screen readers understand them.

  4. Step 4

    Publish and check your site on Google

    Publish your changes, then add your site to Google Search Console so you can see how it appears and how people find it. New sites can take a few days or longer to show up in search results.
