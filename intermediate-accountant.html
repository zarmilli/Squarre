About the Opportunity

Squarredesk is looking for a detail-oriented, organised and financially proficient Intermediate Accountant to join our growing finance department. The successful candidate will be responsible for overseeing the company's day-to-day financial operations, maintaining accurate financial records, managing payroll processes and ensuring that financial transactions are properly recorded, reconciled and reported. Working under the guidance of the Senior Wealth & Financial Advisor, you will play an important role in maintaining the financial integrity of the organisation and ensuring that management has access to reliable financial information for operational and strategic decision-making. We are looking for someone who combines strong accounting knowledge with practical financial management skills, attention to detail and a commitment to maintaining sound financial practices. This is an opportunity to become part of a growing technology company and contribute to establishing structured, efficient and scalable financial operations.

Job Description

As our Intermediate Accountant, you will be responsible for managing the company's daily accounting activities, payroll administration, financial records and operational reporting.

Your responsibilities will include:

1. Day-to-Day Financial Management

  • Managing and recording daily financial transactions.
  • Monitoring accounts payable and accounts receivable.
  • Processing supplier invoices, payments and customer receipts.
  • Performing regular bank reconciliations and account reconciliations.
  • Monitoring company expenditure and maintaining accurate expense records.
  • Maintaining the general ledger and supporting schedules.
  • Assisting with cash flow tracking and operational financial monitoring.
  • Ensuring that financial transactions are properly documented and categorised.

2. Payroll Administration

  • Managing monthly payroll processing and employee remuneration records.
  • Calculating salaries, overtime, leave balances and applicable deductions.
  • Administering PAYE, UIF and SDL calculations and submissions where applicable.
  • Preparing and maintaining employee payroll records.
  • Ensuring that payroll is processed accurately and within agreed deadlines.
  • Coordinating payroll-related documentation and reporting.
  • Maintaining confidentiality and security of employee financial information.

3. Financial Records & Reporting

  • Maintaining accurate and up-to-date accounting records.
  • Preparing monthly management accounts and financial summaries.
  • Assisting with income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements.
  • Preparing periodic expense, revenue and operational financial reports.
  • Monitoring discrepancies and investigating irregular financial transactions.
  • Maintaining organised financial documentation for audits and statutory requirements.
  • Assisting with the preparation of annual financial statements and supporting schedules.

4. Taxation & Regulatory Compliance

  • Assisting with VAT calculations, reconciliations and submissions where applicable.
  • Preparing and maintaining supporting documentation for SARS submissions.
  • Monitoring tax-related deadlines and ensuring timely preparation of required information.
  • Supporting statutory reporting and financial compliance processes.
  • Liaising with external accountants, auditors and tax practitioners when required.
  • Keeping informed of relevant changes in accounting standards and tax legislation.

5. Internal Financial Controls

  • Implementing and maintaining appropriate financial recordkeeping procedures.
  • Monitoring transaction accuracy and identifying potential accounting discrepancies.
  • Maintaining proper supporting documentation for financial transactions.
  • Assisting with the development of financial policies and accounting procedures.
  • Supporting internal and external audit processes.
  • Ensuring that financial records are maintained in accordance with applicable accounting standards.

6. Financial Department Coordination

  • Reporting directly to the Senior Wealth & Financial Advisor.
  • Providing accurate financial information to support treasury and capital allocation decisions.
  • Preparing financial reports and supporting schedules for management review.
  • Highlighting unusual expenditure, financial discrepancies and potential cash flow concerns.
  • Assisting with budgeting, forecasting and financial planning exercises.
  • Supporting the broader finance department in maintaining efficient financial operations.

Role Requirements

Candidates should meet the following requirements:

  • A bachelor's degree, diploma or recognised qualification in Accounting, Financial Accounting, Finance or a related field.
  • At least 2–4 years of practical accounting or financial administration experience.
  • Working knowledge of general accounting principles and financial reporting.
  • Experience with accounts payable, accounts receivable and bank reconciliations.
  • Practical experience in payroll administration and salary processing.
  • Understanding of South African taxation, including PAYE, UIF, SDL and VAT.
  • Familiarity with SARS compliance requirements and statutory financial obligations.
  • Ability to prepare financial statements, management accounts and financial reports.
  • Strong numerical, analytical and problem-solving abilities.
  • High attention to detail and accuracy in financial recordkeeping.
  • Good organisational and time-management skills.
  • Ability to handle confidential financial and employee information.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to work within a structured reporting environment.

Good to Have

The following qualifications, skills and experience will be advantageous:

  • Professional accounting credentials or part-qualification with SAIPA, SAICA, CIMA or another recognised professional body.
  • Experience with accounting software such as Sage, Xero, QuickBooks or similar platforms.
  • Familiarity with South African payroll systems and HR administration platforms.
  • Experience preparing monthly management accounts and financial statements.
  • Knowledge of IFRS and IFRS for SMEs.
  • Experience assisting with annual audits and tax submissions.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills, including financial formulas, pivot tables and reporting.
  • Experience working within a technology company, SaaS business or ecommerce environment.
  • Familiarity with budgeting, cash flow forecasting and financial modelling.
  • Experience establishing accounting procedures and improving financial workflows.
